      Career Hub

      Free guides
      Resume

      Build a one-page fresher resume

      Put education, projects, skills and internships first. Add one line of proof for each skill, such as a project or certificate.

      Interview

      Prepare for HR and technical rounds

      Practise "tell me about yourself", explain your final-year project clearly, and revise the basics of the skills listed in the job.

      Safety

      Spot a fake job offer

      Real companies never ask for money. Check the apply link is on the company's own site or a known hiring portal, and match the job ID.

      Process

      Typical fresher selection rounds

      Online test (aptitude and coding or domain), then a technical interview, then an HR interview. Some roles add a group discussion.

      FAQs

      Does FreshJobs charge any fee?

      No. FreshJobs is free. We only share openings and you apply directly on the company's own website.

      Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

      No. The resume checker reads your file inside your own browser. If you tap "Get AI resume tips", the resume text is sent to the AI only to write your tips.

      Is FreshJobs the employer?

      No. We are not the employer or a recruitment agency. Each job page links to the official hiring page.

      A job link is not working. What should I do?

      The company may have closed the opening. Openings can close anytime, so apply early.

      FRESHJOBS

      FreshJobs shares job openings for freshers and early-career candidates. We are not the employer or a recruitment agency, and we never charge candidates. Always apply on the company's official page.
